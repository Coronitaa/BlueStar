--- 1. DESCRIPTION ---
BlueStar is an all-in-one desktop game management and launcher for modern PC gaming.

Key Features:
• Manage game instances from multiple sources (Steam, local folders, DepotBox).
• Ultra-fast offline catalog with instant search and community ratings.
• Integrated depot downloader with manifest verification.
• Online multiplayer via Steamworks and offline LAN multiplayer with zero Steam dependency.
• 1-Click DLC unlocking and integrated mod management (Unity, Unreal).
• Automatic Windows prerequisites scanner and installer (VC++, DirectX, .NET).
