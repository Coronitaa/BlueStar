## BlueStar v1.6.6

### What's New & Improved
* **ReFix v2.6.5 Integration:** Updated the bundled ReFix AutoDeploy, Uninstaller, and DLC Unlocker scripts (Windows & Linux) to v2.6.5, adding support for Epic Games Store and Microsoft Store / Xbox PC titles without UAC admin elevation prompts or automatic Steam shortcut scripts.
* **Epic Games & Microsoft Store Import:** Added scanning, importing, and background verification for installed Epic Games Store and Microsoft Store titles directly from Home, Library, and the sidebar.
* **Faster Internal Browsers:** Optimized the embedded Store, Steam Workshop, and Nexus Mods browsers by sharing a pre-warmed lightweight Chromium WebView2 environment, blocking ad/analytics trackers, caching Steam cookie sync, and lowering memory usage when browser panels are hidden.
* **Steam Import Icon:** Updated the "Import Installed Steam Games" buttons and modal headers to display the Steam icon instead of a generic gamepad icon.
